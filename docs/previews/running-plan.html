Is the Hostinger KVM 8 enough as the main server?

Good for

  • The Kynex app and API server, in Mumbai
  • Staging, automations, scheduled jobs, the GitHub test runner
  • Making voice clips on the processor (Kokoro)
  • Good value: 32 GB RAM for about ₹2,200 a month on the promotional term

Not on its own for

  • The only copy of patient data. One server is a single point of failure. Patient data needs a managed database with automatic backups, plus a second copy elsewhere.
  • Years of video and call recordings. 400 GB fills up within months; object storage is cheaper and safer.
  • AI that needs a graphics card (Maya2, video models): it has no GPU.

Recommended setup

The VPS runs the app. A managed database in Mumbai holds patient records. AWS S3 in Mumbai holds files and backups, with a second copy in Hyderabad. Bunny Stream delivers the instructor videos. Everything stays in India.

Keeping patient data for years

How long

Indian medical-records rules ask clinics to keep patient records for at least 3 years. The DPDP Act then requires deleting personal data once it's no longer needed, unless a law requires keeping it. Proposed policy: keep everything for 3 years after a patient's last visit, then delete it or remove identifying details. QI Spine's legal team should confirm this.

Cost grows slowly

Older files move to Deep Archive at about $2 per TB a month. Three years of video for 1,000 patients (about 15 TB) costs roughly $30 a month to keep. Getting an old file back takes hours and costs a little.

Change from today

Today patients' exercise videos stay on their phones. Storing them centrally needs the patient's clear consent in the app, encryption, and access logs. The 3D reports need no extra storage: they're drawn from the movement data we already save.

People after launch

Why someone is needed

Someone has to watch the servers and backups, apply security updates, handle WhatsApp/SMS template approvals, answer clinic issues, and ship fixes. Claude speeds up the coding, but someone still has to be responsible for the live system.

Options

  • Lean: you plus Claude Max, with a freelance full-stack/DevOps developer on a monthly retainer for on-call and security.
  • Steady: one full-time developer (with their own Claude seat) and a part-time QA/support person.

Get quotes; this will be larger than all the software costs combined.
